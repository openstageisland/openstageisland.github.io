  <section id="welcome" class="guide-section" data-guide-section aria-labelledby="welcome-heading">
    <h2 id="welcome-heading">Hey there Islander!</h2>
    <p class="guide-lede">Welcome to Open Stage Island!</p>
    <p>
      If you chose to do so already &mdash; great to have you in our Open Stage
      Island team. Read this guide to discover more about us and how we see you
      fit. Thank you for your interest and dedication!
    </p>
    <p>
      OSI is a musical venue on moderate mainland in a nature setting surrounded
      by water, accessible on foot, per teleport, by boat and even with a
      helicopter via the adjacent sim &mdash; an interactive experience for
      artists &amp; audience, including neighbors. We're located close to
      Infohubs because we love newbies. Many artists starting here get picked up
      by other venues.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section id="music" class="guide-section" data-guide-section aria-labelledby="music-heading">
    <h2 id="music-heading">&#9835; Music</h2>
    <p>
      Our focus lies on electronic music, this can vary from relaxing lounge all
      the way to the filthiest dubstep and hardest teknobeats. Curious if your
      musical preference can be played live? Check out this non-exhaustive list:
    </p>
    <p class="guide-callout">
      house, techno, reggae, psy dub, psytrance, goatrance, tekno, drum n bass,
      dubstep, funkot, electro, trap, breaks, experimental, minimal, tech, deep,
      darkwave, hardstyle, breakcore, jungle, liquid dnb, hi tech...
    </p>
    <p>
      <strong>Only live produced/mixed sets are allowed</strong>, including
      experimental and prepared performances, not a replay of your recording.
      Your prerecorded sets do not belong in a livestream at Open Stage Island
      (we already have radio, thanks). Do not fool yourself and your audience.
    </p>
    <p>
      You can promote yourself on Discord where there is a channel provided for
      this purpose &mdash; see <a href="#estate">Estate</a> for how to reach it.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section id="open-stage" class="guide-section" data-guide-section aria-labelledby="open-stage-heading">
    <h2 id="open-stage-heading">&#127881; Open Stage</h2>
    <p>
      You don't need permission &mdash; connect and show us your skills! We have
      a hybrid concept where dj's that like to play spontaneously and those
      engaging in an organised, predetermined event can both perform. Different
      (or even the same) artists like different types of performances.
    </p>
    <p>
      Open Stage Island is also offering reservable timeslots; we've a weekly
      lineup roster &mdash; you can play without scheduling!
    </p>
    <p>
      Experience has taught us this is the most popular method of bringing live
      electronic music artists to an audience, catering to requests or inabilities
      to plan fixed moments. Everybody gets a chance at performing live.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section id="reservations" class="guide-section" data-guide-section aria-labelledby="reservations-heading">
    <h2 id="reservations-heading">&#128197; Reservations</h2>
    <p>
      If you're going for a <strong>recurrent weekly gig</strong> &mdash; please
      contact us if we haven't reached out to you already. We'll make a gcalendar
      event with your information; please provide your email address and some
      link to your earlier mixing.
    </p>
    <p>
      If you're planning a <strong>single time event</strong>, use the
      reservations desk to book a free spot automatically in our timetable. This
      way, you can claim a free spot whenever you want. (It also calculates your
      own timezone for easier planning.
      <em>Warning: until we have reviewed your reservation, your email address
      will be public (!)</em>)
    </p>
    <p class="guide-note">
      Both routes are self-service: the
      <a href="http://timetable.openstageis.land" rel="noopener noreferrer">weekly
      timetable</a> shows what is already booked, and the
      <a href="http://reservations.openstageis.land" rel="noopener noreferrer">reservation
      desk</a> claims a free slot and works out your timezone automatically.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section id="streaming" class="guide-section" data-guide-section aria-labelledby="streaming-heading">
    <h2 id="streaming-heading">&#128250; Streaming</h2>
    <p>
      If you have questions about connecting &amp; playing or mixing live at Open
      Stage Island, ask in the usual channels &mdash; the streaming guide covers
      it in full. You can play with a stream we provide!
    </p>
    <p>
      As you set your stream (or somewhere when you start if using our
      <strong>shared stream</strong>) a dialog will appear: choose the music
      style closest to what you will be playing that set. Thanks for helping
      inform people more efficiently!
    </p>
  </section>

  <section id="hosting" class="guide-section" data-guide-section aria-labelledby="hosting-heading">
    <h2 id="hosting-heading">&#129309; Hosting</h2>
    <p>
      Promoters are welcome, both working with a DJ as independent hosts. Invite
      some friends over, greet new visitors, inform about OSI in other groups
      (chat / notices). Introduce new dj's!
    </p>
    <p>
      Make sure that you only post a notification <strong>one time</strong> in
      group per set. DJ's often post a notification themselves, so double check!
    </p>
    <p>
      For hosts &amp; promoters there is a diamond tip jar behind the stage at the
      arcade, next to the generator. Click it and the tipjar will float above you
      until you log out or leave. <strong>17% of host tips are commission for
      Open Stage Island.</strong>
    </p>
    <p>
      There is <strong>no commission on tips for djs</strong>, they always will
      receive 100% of the tips.
    </p>
  </section>

  <section id="faq" class="guide-section" data-guide-section aria-labelledby="faq-heading">
    <h2 id="faq-heading">&#10067; FAQ</h2>

    <div class="guide-qa">
      <h3>Can I change stage decoration?</h3>
      <p>
        Some artists might want to change things up a bit. You can click the front
        LED triangle screens to cycle through textures. If you keep complexity and
        texture area low enough, you can also attach some decorative party
        attachments on yourself, or rez some (autoreturn few minutes after you leave).
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="guide-qa">
      <h3>How do I lower lag?</h3>
      <p>There are a few important ways to reduce individual and overall lag:</p>
      <ul>
        <li>Reduce draw distance to minimum range</li>
        <li>Drop your graphical settings when there are many people</li>
        <li>Lower maximum allowed avatars</li>
        <li>Reduce the allowed particles</li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        Another important element is the 3D complexity and script count of your
        avatar and attachments. Remove scripts you do not use (but first make a
        copy of those objects in your inventory) and you will immediately see a
        significant drop in lag.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="guide-qa">
      <h3>Why do I hear annoying (repetitive) sounds throughout the music?</h3>
      <p>
        You can lower or even mute audio channels with separate volume for Buttons,
        Ambient, Sounds &amp; Media on all SL viewers. Also allowing gestures of
        everyone for ambiance &mdash; this is the audio channel 'Sounds' so you can
        mute it yourself.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="guide-qa">
      <h3>Is voice activated? How rude!</h3>
      <p>
        When there is no dj playing, voice will be enabled. Voice is automatically
        disabled when a dj starts to stream!
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="guide-qa">
      <h3>If this is an open stage, why would you even have scheduled timeslots?</h3>
      <p>
        We love our hybrid concept! Not every artist is the same: we're a growing
        crew of artists with different preferences; for some announcing &amp;
        planning their gig ahead with a fix time is crucial to their performance,
        others are much better off spontaneous &mdash; only when they feel like it.
      </p>
      <p>
        It allows scheduled artists to come back whenever they want and play some
        more throughout the week!
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="guide-qa">
      <h3>I am not a dj, can I still help?</h3>
      <p>
        Yes you can! Outside of our amazing crew of artists providing great sound
        experiences for guests, there are promoters that also greet/host AND we are
        looking for an event manager.
      </p>
      <p>Talk to us!</p>
    </div>
  </section>
